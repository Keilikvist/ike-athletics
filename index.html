Mission Statement

We, the members of the Eisenhower Senior High community, are committed to enriching the lives of all whom we touch. We will accomplish this goal by maintaining academic excellence, examining and sharing positive values, affirming dignity in diversity, promoting positive social development, and serving as a community resource.

The Word "Coach" is a short, but powerful word!
